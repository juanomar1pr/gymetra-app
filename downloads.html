    Download

    Download Gymetra Host on your Mac and Gymetra Judge on your judges' iPhones or iPads.

    Open the macOS installer and drag Gymetra Host to Applications. Launch the app from your Dock.

    Activate a free 2-week trial inside Gymetra Host and start running competitions immediately. Enter your license key inside Gymetra Host to unlock full competition features. No account required.
